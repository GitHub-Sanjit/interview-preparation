# Developer Performance System (30 Days)

## 1. Goal

This system is designed to help you:

* Stay physically active while coding 10–12+ hours/day
* Avoid neck, back, and wrist pain
* Maintain high focus during deep work
* Prevent mental fatigue and procrastination loops
* Build long-term developer stamina

---

# 3. 30-Day Fatigue-Proof Developer Body Routine

## 🧱 Weekly Structure

### Week 1–2: Adaptation Phase

Focus: avoid soreness, build habit

### Week 3–4: Endurance Phase

Focus: energy stability + posture strength

---

# 🔥 Daily Workout (30–40 min)

## 🟢 Day Type A (Mon/Wed/Fri) — Strength + Stability

* Squats × 15
* Push-ups × 10
* Lunges × 10 each leg
* Plank × 30–45 sec
* Glute bridge × 15
* Wall sit × 30 sec

👉 2–3 rounds

---

## 🔵 Day Type B (Tue/Thu/Sat) — Low Cardio Flow

* March in place × 2 min
* Step touch × 2 min
* High knees (slow) × 1 min
* Arm swings × 1 min
* Light shadow movement × 2 min

👉 Repeat 2–3 rounds

---

## 🟡 Sunday — Recovery Day

* Cat-cow × 10
* Child’s pose × 2 min
* Neck + shoulder release
* Slow breathing 5 min

---

# 4. Focus Training System (Deep Work Stamina)

## 🎯 Rule 1: 90-Minute Focus Cycles

Work in cycles:

* 90 min deep work
* 10 min break

Repeat.

---

## 🧠 Focus Build Method

### Phase 1 (Days 1–7)

* 60 min focus only
* 10 min break

### Phase 2 (Days 8–21)

* 90 min focus cycles

### Phase 3 (Days 22–30)

* 2 × 120 min deep work blocks

---

## ⚡ Focus Activation Routine (Before Work)

Do this before every deep session:

* Sit still for 30 sec
* Deep breathing × 10
* Say:

> “I only need to start. Not finish.”

---

## 🚫 Anti-Distraction Protocol

During focus:

* Phone out of reach
* No YouTube/shorts tabs
* Only 1 task open

If distracted:

> Stand → breathe → restart in 30 sec

---

## 🔋 Mental Fatigue Recovery

If brain feels tired:

Do 1 cycle:

* Stand up
* 20 jumping jacks OR marching
* Drink water
* 10 deep breaths

👉 resets focus in under 2 minutes

---

# 5. Key Performance Rules

## Rule 1: Movement = Mandatory

Even on busy days:

* Minimum 5 min movement every 90 min

---

## Rule 2: Never Sit More Than 90 Minutes

Break cycles always.

---

## Rule 3: Energy First, Discipline Second

If energy is low:

* reduce intensity
* never skip completely

---

## Rule 4: Consistency > Intensity

Small daily movement beats heavy irregular workouts.
