# Brain Reset System (7-Day Detox + Phone Control + Focus Tracker)

---

# 🧠 PART 1: 7-DAY DOPAMINE DETOX RESET PLAN

## 🎯 Goal

Reset your brain’s reward system so:

* Phone stops feeling “compulsive”
* Focus becomes easier
* Urges reduce naturally
* Deep work feels normal again

---

## 🚫 RULES FOR ALL 7 DAYS

* No social media (YouTube Shorts, Facebook, Instagram)
* No porn
* No random browsing
* No phone in bed
* No scrolling after 9 PM

---

## 🔁 REPLACEMENT RULE (VERY IMPORTANT)

Every time you feel urge:

DO ONE:

* Walk 2–5 minutes
* Drink water
* 10 push-ups
* Open book (non-fiction)
* Write 5 lines in journal

👉 You are not “removing habits”
👉 You are “redirecting dopamine”

---

# 📅 DAY-BY-DAY PLAN

---

## DAY 1 — Awareness Day

Focus:

* Observe your addiction patterns

Tasks:

* Track every time you pick up phone
* Write triggers (bored, stress, tired)
* 20 min reading (Atomic Habits or similar)

---

## DAY 2 — Control Day

Focus:

* Add friction

Tasks:

* Delete non-essential apps
* Turn off all notifications
* Phone stays away during work blocks

---

## DAY 3 — Disruption Day

Focus:

* Break automatic habits

Tasks:

* 2-hour phone-free block (morning)
* Replace scrolling with walking

---

## DAY 4 — Replacement Day

Focus:

* Build new dopamine source

Tasks:

* 30 min reading
* 30 min writing/journaling
* Light workout

---

## DAY 5 — Discipline Day

Focus:

* Resist urges intentionally

Tasks:

* When urge comes → delay 10 minutes
* No phone after 9 PM strict

---

## DAY 6 — Deep Work Day

Focus:

* Train focus stamina

Tasks:

* 2 × 90 min deep work sessions
* Phone outside room entire day

---

## DAY 7 — Reset Integration Day

Focus:

* Stabilize new identity

Tasks:

* Reflect:

  * What changed?
  * When urges reduced?
* Plan next week rules

# 📵 PART 2: PHONE ADDICTION HARD BLOCKER SYSTEM

## 🧱 LEVEL 1 — ENVIRONMENT LOCK (MOST IMPORTANT)

### DO THIS TODAY:

* Phone charges OUTSIDE bedroom
* No phone within arm’s reach while working
* Keep phone in another room during deep work

---

## 🧱 LEVEL 2 — DIGITAL LOCKS

### Turn ON:

* App timers (30–60 min max)
* Focus mode during:

  * 8 AM – 2 PM
  * 5 PM – 9 PM

### Block:

* YouTube Shorts
* Instagram Reels
* TikTok (if installed)

---

## 🧱 LEVEL 3 — FRICTION SYSTEM

Make scrolling “annoying”:

* Log out of social apps daily
* Remove saved passwords
* Disable autoplay videos

👉 Your brain stops choosing easiest dopamine

---

## 🧱 LEVEL 4 — EMERGENCY OVERRIDE RULE

When you STILL try to scroll:

Do this immediately:

* Stand up
* Leave room
* 10 deep breaths
* 20 squats OR walk 2 min

👉 Interrupts compulsive loop
