# 30-Day Full-Stack Developer Preparation Guide

## Mission Statement

For the next 30 days, focus on **consistent execution**, not perfection.

Your goal is to become interview-ready by strengthening five pillars:

1. Resume Projects
2. DSA (Data Structures & Algorithms)
3. Interview Preparation
4. Mock Interviews & Communication
5. Physical & Mental Fitness

---

# Core Rules

## Rule 1: Never Miss Two Days in a Row

Bad days happen.

Two bad days become a habit.

---

## Rule 2: Progress > Perfection

One DSA problem deeply understood is better than ten memorized.

---

## Rule 3: Treat Preparation Like a Job

Show up whether motivated or not.

---

## Rule 4: Identity Shift

Replace:

> "I'm trying to become a Full-Stack Developer."

With:

> "I am becoming a Full-Stack Developer."

# Learning Method

For each topic:

## Study (60–90 mins)

Answer:

* What is it?
* Why is it used?
* When should it be used?
* What are the trade-offs?

---

## Teach Back (15–20 mins)

Record a 2-minute video explaining the concept.

Examples:

* React Reconciliation
* JWT Authentication
* SQL Indexing
* SSR vs CSR
* Event Loop
* Middleware

Benefits:

* Communication skills
* Interview confidence
* Better retention

# Indoor Workout Plan (30 Minutes Daily)

Purpose:

* Improve blood circulation
* Reduce stress
* Counteract prolonged sitting
* Improve energy levels

---

## Monday, Wednesday, Friday (Cardio + Strength)

### Warm-Up (5 mins)

* Neck rotations × 10
* Shoulder rolls × 10
* Arm circles × 20
* Hip rotations × 10
* March in place × 2 mins

---

### Main Workout (20 mins)

Perform 3 rounds:

* Bodyweight Squats × 15
* Push-ups × 10
* Glute Bridges × 15
* Mountain Climbers × 20 seconds
* Lunges × 10 each leg
* Plank × 30 seconds

Rest:

45 seconds between rounds.

---

### Cool Down (5 mins)

* Hamstring stretch
* Chest stretch
* Child's pose
* Deep breathing

---

## Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday (Cardio Focus)

### Warm-Up (5 mins)

Light marching.

---

### Cardio Circuit (20 mins)

Repeat 4 rounds:

* Jumping Jacks × 45 seconds
* High Knees × 30 seconds
* March in Place × 45 seconds
* Step Touch × 45 seconds

Rest:

30 seconds.

Low-impact option:

Replace jumping with marching.

---

### Mobility (5 mins)

* Cat-Cow Stretch
* Hip Flexor Stretch
* Thoracic Rotations
* Seated Forward Fold

---

## Sunday (Recovery Day)

20–30 minutes:

* Light stretching
* Gentle yoga
* Relaxed walk

Goal:

Recover, don't exhaust yourself.
